Dataset Schema & Variable Dictionary
Demographic & Background
  • school: GP (Gabriel Pereira), MS (Mousinho da Silveira)
  • sex: Student sex (F: female, M: male)
  • age: Student age (15 to 22)
  • address: Home address type (U: urban, R: rural)
  • famsize: Family size (LE3: ≤3, GT3: >3)
  • Pstatus: Cohabitation status (T: together, A: apart)
  • Medu / Fedu: Parent education level (0 to 4)
  • Mjob / Fjob: Parent occupation category
Academic & School Environment
  • traveltime: Travel time (1: <15m, 2: 15-30m, 3: 30-60m, 4: >1h)
  • studytime: Weekly study time (1: <2h, 2: 2-5h, 3: 5-10h, 4: >10h)
  • failures: Past class failures (0 to 4)
  • schoolsup: Extra educational school support (yes/no)
  • famsup: Family educational support (yes/no)
  • paid: Extra paid classes within math (yes/no)
  • higher: Desires higher education (yes/no)
  • internet: Internet access at home (yes/no)
Social, Health & Targets
  • famrel: Family relationships quality (1 to 5)
  • freetime: Free time after school (1 to 5)
  • goout: Going out with peers (1 to 5)
  • Dalc / Walc: Workday / Weekend alcohol use (1 to 5)
  • health: Current health status (1 to 5)
  • absences: Number of school absences (0 to 93)
  • G1, G2: Excluded from early-warning features
  • G3: Final math grade target (0 to 20)
