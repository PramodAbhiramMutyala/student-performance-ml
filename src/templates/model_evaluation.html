Methodology & Ethical Limitations
Early-Warning Feature Isolation

To build a genuine early-warning system, G1 (First Period) and G2 (Second Period) grades were excluded from model input features. This prevents circular data leakage and tests true baseline predictive capability from socio-academic indicators.

Model Limitations

The dataset comprises 395 students from two Portuguese secondary schools. Findings reflect this specific cohort and should not be generalized across disparate educational cultures without recalibration.

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Predictions are decision-support aids for mentors and educators, not deterministic student evaluations. High-stakes academic actions require comprehensive human assessment.
